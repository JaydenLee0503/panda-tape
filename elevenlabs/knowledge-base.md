## What Panda Tape is
Panda Tape is a **compostable kinesio (kinesiology) tape** — the stretchy
therapeutic tape athletes and physios use to support joints and muscles. Unlike
conventional kinesio tape (plastic film + acrylic glue that can persist ~100
years in landfill), Panda Tape is made from plant materials and is certified
**home-compostable in 12 weeks**.

Tagline: **Stick. Sweat. Compost.**

## Materials
- **Backing:** 100% bamboo lyocell weave — four-way stretch, breathable, softer
  than cotton, spun from FSC-certified bamboo.
- **Adhesive:** Seaweed-based adhesive. Holds through up to five sweaty days.
  Zero acrylics, zero latex, zero irritation.
- **Zero microplastics.** Nothing plastic in the product.

## Compostability & disposal
- Certified **home-compostable** — breaks down in about **12 weeks**.
- Disposal options:
  1. Bury a used strip in a home garden bed / home compost.
  2. Drop it at a **Panda point** (compost bins and partner gyms mapped in the app).
- "Recyclable" is not the same as recycled — under 9% of plastic actually gets
  recycled. Panda Tape avoids that problem by composting instead.

## What it's used for (body areas & conditions)
- **Knees:** runner's knee, jumper's knee, meniscus support, post-op recovery.
- **Shoulders:** rotator cuff, swimmer's shoulder, posture assist.
- **Ankles & feet:** sprains, plantar fasciitis, Achilles support.
- **Elbows & wrists:** tennis elbow, climber's wrist, keyboard strain.
- **Back & core:** lower-back support, lifting days, posture cueing.
- **Calves & shins:** shin splints, calf strains, race-day support.

## The Panda app (drop-off)
- Maps compost bins and partner gyms near you so a used strip never hits landfill.
- Workflow: **peel it, ball it, drop it** — the app logs every strip you return to soil.
- Currently **312 drop-off bins and counting**.

## Sustainability context (why it exists)
- ~5,000 tonnes of kinesio tape is landfilled every year.
- Conventional tape is plastic film + acrylic glue and effectively never breaks
  down — every roll ever made still exists.
